# Inez na łowach

A one-level maze-chase mini game starring Inez (from *Kaczor do sauny!*): her head roams a neon maze eating guys while angry bad carbs (pizza, donut, bread, fries) chase her. Eating a "ciacho" (a hunk, the four big pick-ups) turns the tables for 7 seconds, so Inez can eat the carbs (zero calories). Eat all 205 guys to win; three lives.

- Controls: arrows / WASD, swipe or on-screen arrows on phones, P pause, M mute.
- Each carb has its own chase style: pizza goes straight for Inez, the donut aims ahead of her, the bread flanks using the pizza's position, and the fries chase only from afar.
- Art: Gemini (Inez's head, carbs, guys, the hunk). Voice: ElevenLabs (Magda, the same voice as Inez in *Kaczor do sauny!*). Music and some effects come from *Kaczor do sauny!*.
- Single static `index.html`, no build. Best score is kept in `localStorage`.
