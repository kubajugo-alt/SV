# Świąteczna Przygoda Kaczorów

Part 3 of the Christmas trilogy (after the Kaka living-room game in `kaka/` and *Kaczor do sauny!* in `kaczor-do-sauny/`). A horizontal side-scrolling shoot 'em up: Kaka, Kaczor and their 4-year-old son Mikołaj fly a sleigh pulled by Rudolf over a snowy Polish village at night, dropping presents into chimneys while Inez, Gayzilla and the Grinch try to stop them from small planes.

## Play
- Arrows / WASD fly the sleigh · Space / J: Kaczor throws snowballs · X / K: Mikołaj drops a present · C / L: Rudolf's nose sends out waves of red light that shoot down every plane they reach, wipe out enemy shots and hit the boss hard (2 per level) · P pause · M mute.
- Phones: drag anywhere to steer, on-screen buttons for snowball, present and Rudolf's nose; subtitles move to the top.
- Presents drift back with the ground, so drop one when a chimney is just under the sleigh; the chimney marker turns gold ("TERAZ!") when a drop would land in it. A delivered house lights up and its chimney smokes. Hitting the very centre of a chimney scores a bonus.
- A plane that is shot down vanishes in a cloud of dust and its pilot floats away on a parachute (goblins and the Grinch on patched olive ones, rebel elves on striped ones, Inez on pink hearts); shot ravens tumble down dazed, shedding feathers. Bosses escape the same way.
- Shot-down planes sometimes drop power-ups: candy cane (triple snowballs), cookie with milk (+1 heart), star (shield).
- Stars at the end of a level: 1 for finishing, 2 for ≥50% of the chimneys, 3 for ≥75%.

## Prologue: Wigilia wieczorem
Part 3 is Kaczor's dream on Christmas Eve. "Graj" first opens the Kaczors' living room from part 2 (`kaka/`, its room, sofa, tree and sprites reused) at 21:00: Kaka, done with all the preparations, gives Kaczor one single job — put the presents under the tree at midnight — Kaczor promises to "just close his eyes for a moment", little Mikołaj asks whether Santa will make it before midnight, and Kaczor mumbles "Rudolf… sleigh…" and falls asleep. The room dissolves into white ("…i Kaczorowi śni się Wigilijna noc") and level 1 starts. "Pomiń" skips it.

## Zamieć (all shoot 'em up levels)
Every half a minute or so a blizzard sweeps over the sky and covers the screen; only the glow around Rudolf's nose shows the way, and C (the nose's wave of light) blows it away — the blizzard always leaves at least one charge for that.

## Level 1: Nad osiedlem
About 2.5 minutes of houses with flocks of ravens in pink scarves that throw stolen Christmas baubles (sine waves, dives with bauble volleys, lines), then a boss fight with the Great Raven (crown, pink boa, a sack of stolen baubles): bauble fans, homing streams, a dash at the sleigh and, once angry, a rain of baubles. As he flees he promises to tell "the boss" everything.

## Cutscene: Skraj lasu
After the Great Raven flees, "Dalej" lands the sleigh on a clearing at the edge of the forest. Maryańska (the hippie mentor from *Kaczor do sauny!*, her Ludo animations and voice) waits by her tent and campfire and sends the family into the forest; Kaczor, Kaka and Mikołaj answer. "Pomiń" skips it. Maryańska then invites them into her tent.

## Maryańska's tent
After the landing Maryańska invites the family into her tent (a hub like the living room in the Kaka game). Three glowing spots start three Christmas mini-games, and each finished one changes the tent:
- **Pierniki z ziołami** (stove and table): cut six gingerbread cookies out of the dough, then bake three trays by stopping the oven needle in the golden zone. A plate of iced cookies appears on the table.
- **Łapacz snów** (hook over the entrance): Maryańska shows a sequence of beads, feathers and cones; repeat it (3, 4, then 5 items). The dream catcher and two garlands of dried oranges appear.
- **Kolęda na gitarze** (guitar): "Cicha noc" (Silent Night, public domain) falls on three strings; hit the notes with A S D (or J K L, arrows, taps) for plucked-string sounds while Maryańska strums the bass; 60% of the notes passes. The fairy lights light up.
"Pomiń minigry" goes straight to level 2; when all three are done Maryańska sends the family off.

## Level 2: Leśne gobliny
A night forest. Presents go to beaver lodges (stick chimney) and badger setts (stone chimney pipe); a delivered home's animal pops out hugging its present and thanks the family. Tall snowy trees stand between the homes: their crowns hurt the sleigh (crown shapes in `art/trees.json`), so the sleigh flies high and dives between trees to drop presents. Green goblins in rusty planes throw lumps of coal, some of them in arcs; the boss is the Grinch in his patched plane, throwing coal. Level 2 can also be picked on the title screen once unlocked (or with `?level=2`). Test links: `?tent` opens Maryańska's tent, `?cut` plays the landing cutscene.

## Jugo's gingerbread cottage
After the Grinch, "Dalej" lands the sleigh at the edge of the village, in front of Jugo's rainbow gingerbread cottage. Jugo invites the family in: Gayzilla, in a headscarf and patchwork apron, stirs a bubbling cauldron like Baba Jaga and spoils Santa, whom both of them call "Papi" (hearts drift between them, Jugo holds mistletoe). Three glowing spots start three mini-games:
- **Kociołek Gayzilli** (cauldron): move the cauldron (mouse, touch, ← →) and catch ten recipe items (porcini, beetroot, gingerbread); rotten eggs and smelly socks spoil the soup, three of them start it over. The steam turns green and cheerful.
- **Karmienie Papiego** (armchair): Papi sways in his armchair; click or tap to throw pierogi and makowiec into his mouth, eight within 40 seconds. A plate of food and more hearts appear.
- **Taniec z Papim** (disco ball): Gayzilla shows arrow steps, repeat them with the arrows or by clicking (3, 4, 5, then 6 steps) while Papi dances along. The disco ball lights up the room.
When all three are done Papi confesses why he is hiding with the boys: Inez was the new Mrs. Claus, the romance failed, and she turned the elves against him and took over the North Pole (Kaka works out that Inez sent the ravens). Papi decides women are too much trouble and he is staying with the boys, because at least Gayzilla cooks well; Kaka protests, and Papi sends the family to the North Pole. Level 3 follows. Test links: `?village` plays the landing at Jugo's, `?cottage` opens the cottage.

## Level 3: Biegun północny
An aurora night over the ice. Presents go down the chimneys of elf workshops and ice houses flying Inez's pink flags; each delivery frees an elf, who pops out cheering. Rebel elves in candy-striped planes throw snowballs (denser waves than before). There is no boss fight yet: at the end Inez flies by in her pink biplane, taunts Kaczor and shoots the sleigh down with a giant heart, and the family crash-lands in front of the elf village. "Dalej" opens the elf village platformer. Test link: `?level=3` unlocks the level on the title screen.

## The family in the platformer (village and hall)
Tab (or Q, or ⇄ on phones) switches between the three members of the family, in place: **Kaczor** punches (X), throws snowballs (C) and glides; **Kaka** has a double jump and her **checklist** (X) — four seconds in which everything except the family slows down (12 s cooldown); **little Mikołaj** is small enough for one-tile gaps and is the only one who can open the elves' cages. Her second jump is always full height (releasing the key does not cut it short), so a quick tap-tap reliably reaches the lever ledges. On a rope each of the three has a dedicated hanging animation (Gemini pose without rope, 16-frame Ludo swing in `art/anim/*_swing.webp`): fists on the rope, the body turned with the rope's angle, legs swinging faster the faster the swing. Twice per level a **trio puzzle** needs all three: a candy-cane gate whose lever sits on a ledge only Kaka's double jump reaches, a "mouse hole" under a wall that only Mikołaj fits through, and an ice wall that only Kaczor's punch breaks. Portraits under the hearts show who is active; switching to a bigger character where there is no headroom is refused.

## Magazyn zabawek (`magazyn/`, top-down stealth)
Between the production hall and the bobsled: a maze of toy shelves seen from above. Pink cameras sweep and elf guards with torches patrol (their vision cones are cast against the shelves); getting seen fills a "?" → "!" meter and sets off the alarm — the guards run at Kaczor and wind-up toy planes home in. Standing still in a cardboard box hides him; Shift is faster but guards nearby hear it. Find the golden key and reach the gate (3 hearts, respawn at the start or at the key).

## Wioska elfów (platformer, `wioska/`)
The elf village is played on foot with the engine of *Kaczor do sauny!* (copied into `wioska/` and reskinned): Kaczor runs, jumps, glides, punches and throws snowballs through a generated village level (one level, snowy platforms, crumbling bridges, springs, icicles, moving platforms). Rebel elves (Ludo animations: walk, throw, hit, dizzy, huff, pant) throw snowballs, wind-up nutcracker soldiers charge along the ground and toy planes swoop; Kaczor comments on each kind. Ten loyal elves sit in candy-cane cages: touch or punch a cage to free the elf, who jumps for joy and thanks him (freed elves skip away hugging their gift — a 16-frame Ludo loop, `art/anim/elf_free.webp`; the wind-up nutcracker marches with his key turning, `toy_march`, and the toy plane flies with a spinning propeller and fluttering flag, `toy_plane`) (counter at the top). Candy canes are the collectibles (25 = +1 heart), hot cocoa gives a heart, a golden star hides on a high ledge, candy-cane lanterns are checkpoints, and Kaka does not chase anyone here. At the end the Elf General (the rebel elf, bigger, in the mini-boss slot) guards the gate of Santa's workshop with snowball volleys, homing snowballs, charges and a snowball rain; he can only be hurt when out of breath or by a stomp. Every hit knocks a mug of hot cocoa out of him (+1 heart). Beyond the gate a card says the village is free but Inez still rules the pole; "Dalej" returns to the main game with `?after=wioska`, which shows a "Ciąg dalszy nastąpi" card (the final battle with Inez comes later). `wioska/?boss` jumps to the General. The title screen of the main game gets a "Wioska elfów" button once level 3 is done.

## Fabryka zabawek: Maryańska and the snow cannon
After the village (`?after=wioska`) the family walks into the toy factory, where Maryańska turns out to be part of the elf resistance against Inez. Three logic puzzles in the workshop each give a part of the weapon, a **snow cannon**:
- **Rury z parą** (pipes on the left wall): rotate pipe tiles until steam flows from the boiler to the compressor (random solvable layout every time) → the steam boiler.
- **Panel celownika** (lamp panel): Lights Out on a 4×4 grid — a press toggles a lamp and its neighbours, light all sixteen → the brass sight.
- **Szyfr do magazynu** (storage door): a Mastermind lock with four different coloured baubles out of six, ten tries, four feedback holes per try (● right place, ○ right colour in the wrong place, empty = colour not in the code) → the snowball hopper. A "💡 Podpowiedź" button lets Maryańska reveal one bauble in its place (up to three). Every one of the 360 codes can be cracked in at most 6 tries by always guessing a combination consistent with the earlier feedback.
In the corner stands an optional pink arcade machine: clicking it opens *Inez na łowach* (Inez's maze-chase game, copied into `arcade/` from `inez-na-lowach/`) in an overlay, and "✕ Wróć do fabryki" returns to the room with the puzzles as they were. The parts land on the workbench and the cannon appears; Maryańska sends Kaczor through the production hall while the others get the bobsled ready. Test link: `?fab`.

## Hala produkcyjna (platformer, `wioska/?level=2`)
The second level of the platformer engine: a toy-factory hall with wooden platforms, lots of springs (also springs on the edge of a pit that throw you across), moving platforms and lifts, and a new mechanic — **swinging ropes** over pits: jump and grab a rope in the air, pump with ◀ ▶ to swing higher, and let go with a jump; the release keeps the swing's speed. Wide pits have two ropes and a crumbling stepping stone. Rebel elves, toy soldiers, toy planes and caged elves as in the village; no boss, the goal is the factory's loading gate. Afterwards `?after=fabryka` shows the bobsled card.

## Pościg w bobslejach (`bobslej/`, Mode 7)
The finale with Inez, on its own Mode 7 engine: a closed ice-channel course (Catmull-Rom loop around the toy factory) is painted into a 2048² ground texture — banked blue channel, red-and-white wall stripe, turbo chevrons, checkered start — and rendered per scanline at 400×225 with a horizon haze, the North Pole sky scrolling with the heading and the camera rolling in curves. Ice blocks line both walls as billboards; trees, candy-cane poles, snowmen and cheering elves stand outside, the start arch spans the track. The family is seen from behind in their red bobsled (Kaczor steers, Kaka, little Mikołaj with the snow cannon at the back) and Inez races ahead in her pink heart bobsled.
- Six zones per lap, each with its own width, ice colour, walls, scenery and light, blending into each other: **Dziedziniec fabryki** (lanterns, elf workshops, a garland banner), **Las choinkowy** (dense pines), **Lodowa jaskinia** (a narrow tunnel of ice arches with glowing crystals, dark sky), **Zamarznięte jezioro** (a very wide, slippery glassy stretch edged with snowbanks, cheering penguins), **Wioska elfów** (narrow streets between houses, snowdrifts across the track that slow you down unless you blast them) and **Skocznia** (two wooden ramps under banners: the sled flies, lands with a thump and a short turbo). The zone name shows on entry.
- Vehicles: three frames each — exactly from behind (default), a slight turn, and side-on when steering hard or sliding (mirrored for the other side); Inez switches frames by how much she curves relative to the camera. The sled rattles with speed, leans into turns, throws snow off the outer side, flies with a shadow over ramps and squashes on landing.
- The channel walls are a continuous 3D strip along both edges, texture-mapped (affine triangles) with honey-brown wooden planks and a Christmas garland of pine, red bows, baubles and fairy lights; darker ice in the cave, low snowbanks on the lake. Trees are complete pines and decorated spruces.
- The crowd: beavers and badgers (as in level 2), reindeer, polar bear cubs, foxes and elf kids, each drawn in two poses (arms up / clapping) and animated — they hop, sway and wave in their own rhythm, go wild when the sled passes close, and all cheer (with a synthesised crowd roar) when a snowball hits Inez or after a good jump. Groups line every zone with a mix that fits it (forest animals in the woods, elves in the village, bears and penguins on the lake), and garland-decked wooden grandstands full of fans stand at the start and at both ramps.
- Snow: the runners throw a constant spray that grows with speed, flies out of the outer side in turns, and gets heavier when braking, sliding, on turbo, landing a jump or ploughing through a drift; soft snow puffs drift behind; Inez's sled kicks up snow too, and snowflakes fall over the whole track.
- Physics: the sled slides a little sideways on the ice, bounces off the channel walls (which slow it and turn the nose away), yellow chevrons give turbo, ▼ brakes for tighter turns.
- Snow cannon: Space / X / ❄ fires arcing snowballs that the brass sight curves towards Inez; ammo is limited (crates on the track give +4).
- Inez: rubber-band racing line, swerves away from snowballs, drops gift boxes and throws hearts back down the track (both spin you out; snowballs pop them). After four hits she speeds up and attacks more often. Eight hits before she finishes three laps win; then `../?after=bobslej` says Inez climbed out of the drift and flew off to her palace, which starts the finale (below); after it comes the ending at Santa's workshop (`?pole`) and then the awakening: the dream fades into the living room at 23:58, Kaka shakes Kaczor awake ("Inez! Bobslej! Armatka śnieżna… To był tylko sen?"), the presents land under the tree just as the clock chimes midnight — with a toy snow cannon among them, exactly like in the dream — Kaka is proud of him, sleepy Mikołaj asks whether Santa came, and the "Wesołych Świąt" card closes the trilogy. Test links: `?wake` (awakening).
- The ground is rendered into a buffer with a margin for the camera roll (not stretched), so every billboard — houses, trees, gates, spectators — stands exactly on its spot of the floor.
- HUD: Inez's hearts, ammo, speed, her lap, distance, timer, minimap; 3-2-1 countdown; synthesised runner noise.

## Character sizes
Rooms with mini-games (tent, cottage, factory) use the scale of the Kaka game: only the host stands in the room at full height (~470 px), the family is the unseen player and talks in subtitles. Cutscenes keep everyone at one standing scale (~390 px) with the sleigh 640 px wide. Platformer levels use the scale of *Kaczor do sauny!*; the Elf General is 1.2× Kaczor like Inez there, caged and freed elves are child-sized.

## Finał: Różowy Pałac
Inez flees with Papi's stolen sack of presents to her palace of pink ice on top of the glacier. The last fight has three phases, each in a different engine:
1. **Poziom 4: Różowy Pałac** (shoot 'em up, a shorter level): elf workshops under the palace, Inez's ravens back in full force (throwing baubles, like everywhere else), then the boss — Inez on her **Pink Reindeer** (fans, streams, dashes, kiss rain, summoned ravens). When its strength runs out comes the **beam duel**: Rudolf's red nose against the reindeer's pink one — mash Space / X / C (or tap the screen) to push the meeting point of the beams to her side before she pushes back; losing a push costs a heart. Inez parachutes into the palace. "Dalej" opens the tower.
2. **Wieża luster** (`wioska/?level=3`, platformer): a pink-ice palace hall with crystal platforms, lifts, springs, crumbling bridges, chandelier chains and caged elves, ending in a duel with the real Inez (her animations from *Kaczor do sauny!*). New attack: the **hall of mirrors** — a simple find-her game: Inez hides inside one of five ornate pink-ice standing mirrors (Gemini art) and every mirror shows an Inez. The fakes are tinted, flickering reflections that throw kisses (painted lips, `art/it_lips.webp`); the real one does not flicker and does not throw. Break mirrors with a punch, snowball or stomp: a fake shatters ("Pusto!", one homing kiss), the real one throws Inez out dizzy for a few hits; if only her mirror is left she falls out on her own, and after 11 s she slips away. A toast explains the rule each time. Tuned to be gentler: Inez has 7 hearts (was 9), stays dazed longer, throws smaller volleys (kisses, kiss rain, homing kisses), the mirror reflections throw less often, and every hit knocks a mug of hot cocoa (+1 heart) out of her, as with the General. Afterwards `../?after=palac` shows the "Wieża zdobyta" card.
3. **Sala tronowa** (`?throne`): Inez makes her last offer — stay with her in the palace, with a sauna, champagne and no shopping lists. Two buttons: "Zostaję w pałacu" (Kaka: "KACZOR!", Mikołaj: "Tato? A prezenty?", Kaczor: "Żartowałem!" — and the question comes back) or "Wracam do rodziny" — Kaczor takes the sack, Inez sulks ("I tak byłeś za stary na mój bobslej!") and wishes him a merry Christmas, the sack rises in a burst of gold.
4. **Finał** (`?finale`): Papi comes back for his sack, names Kaka **Queen of Christmas** (Królowa Świąt) (she takes the heart throne, elves bow), then **Kara dla Inez** (`?kara` jumps straight here): Queen Kaka sentences Inez to a year of wrapping presents in the elves' factory — no phone, no selfies, no pink; the Gemini panel shows Inez in a reindeer sweater with sticky tape in her hair, an elf foreman with a clipboard and her phone in a jar labelled „skonfiskowane”; five new ElevenLabs lines (Kaka, Inez ×2, Kaczor, the elf), lip-synced mouths for Inez and the elf; then, gives little Mikołaj the job of **Santa Claus** (he flies off with Rudolf and the presents), and takes Kaczor to the sauna — both in short towels, Papi still in his Santa hat, and Kaczor can't take his eyes off Papi's biceps. Then the awakening at 23:58.
The title screen gets "Różowy Pałac", "Wieża luster" and "Sala tronowa" buttons as they are reached. New art (Gemini): the palace panorama, Inez on the Pink Reindeer, the mirror-palace hall, the throne room, Inez as the ice queen; voices by ElevenLabs as before.

- Snowballs: a new painted snowball (Gemini, keyed off magenta so the white is not cut away) for the elves in the shoot 'em up and the bobsled, used in the warehouse too; in the platformer the rebel elves now throw snowballs instead of Inez's kisses.
- Direct links: `?papi` plays Papi's story about Inez in the cottage; `?play=1..4` starts a shoot 'em up level, `?boss=1..4` jumps to its boss (`?deer` still works); platformer `wioska/?level=1..3`, with `&boss` straight to the General of the Elves or Inez in the tower.
- Performance (Macs): all pages cap drawing at ~60 fps (ProMotion displays no longer render twice as often), the main game starts at 1.5× resolution instead of 2× on Retina and steps down to 1× if frames run slow, and the star shield uses a canvas shadow instead of stacked CSS filters.

## Assets
- Art by Gemini (`art/`): sleigh with the family and Rudolf in strict side view (Kaczor flies bare-headed), ravens (and the Great Raven), pilots on parachutes, enemy planes (goblin, rebel elf, Inez), houses, animal homes and elf workshops with chimney positions in `houses.json`, seamless night, forest and North Pole panoramas, presents, power-ups and title art. The Grinch is an original grumpy green goblin design.
- Animations by Ludo.ai (`art/anim/`): flying loops for the sleigh, the planes, the ravens and Inez's Pink Reindeer (16 frames each); in the platformer Kaka and Mikołaj have 16-frame run and idle loops like Kaczor, and in the warehouse Kaczor and the elf guards have walking loops.

Single static `index.html`, no build. Best score per level is kept in `localStorage`.
